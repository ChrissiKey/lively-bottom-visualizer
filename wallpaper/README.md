# Bottom Rainbow Audio Visualizer (Lively Wallpaper)

LED-style bar spectrum at the bottom of the screen, no reflection, tuned for bass-heavy music.
Full documentation, presets and updates: https://github.com/ChrissiKey/lively-bottom-visualizer

Based on lively-audio-visualizer by elias123tre: https://github.com/eliasfloreteng/lively-audio-visualizer
License: MIT

Files: index.html, main.js, LivelyProperties.json (settings, English), LivelyProperties.loc.json
(German labels), LivelyInfo.json (metadata), presets/ (preset files), images/ (background images).
